If these numbers change with the feeling but the voice does not, the model is ignoring them rather than the feeling failing to arrive — a different problem with a different fix. eleven_v3 reads the audio tag; eleven_turbo_v2_5 cannot, so no tag is sent to it and the mood rides entirely on the numbers.

The cast

A character with no voice yet is a normal state, not a fault — the voice ids are chosen by hand in the provider’s console and pasted into assets/registry.json. Until one is there, that character stays silent and everything around them carries on.
