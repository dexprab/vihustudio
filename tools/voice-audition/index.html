The feeling

These are the Companions’ own states — the word on the face is the word in the voice. A feeling moves a character’s voice rather than replacing it, so a happy Quill still sounds like Quill.

The model

The two families carry a feeling in opposite ways. eleven_turbo_v2_5 is built for speed and has only the sliders; eleven_v3 is the expressive one and is the only family that reads the audio tags — so on v3 the mood rides on the tag, its stability becomes a three-way choice, and style and speed are not its vocabulary at all. Choosing here changes nothing permanently — it is for listening. Whatever wins gets written into assets/registry.json.

The cast

A character with no voice yet is a normal state, not a fault — the voice ids are chosen by hand in the provider’s console and pasted into assets/registry.json. Until one is there, that character stays silent and everything around them carries on.

Does this voice read tags?

The one test that settles it, and it needs no code. Choose v3 and the feeling neutral (so nothing of ours is prepended), then put a tag in the line above and press Speak on a character. Three outcomes, three different meanings: it whispers → tags work · you hear the word “whispers” → this model is not reading tags · it sounds normal → the tag was accepted and ignored, which is voice-dependent and documented as such.
