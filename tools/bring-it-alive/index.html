Developer · preservation & layers

Deskew is measured and declined, with the reason in the log below — the photographed page is the source, untouched. Lighting is flattened on a decision copy only; the export path reads original pixels exclusively. Regions are DERIVED from the raw mask (never stored): closed areas get independently editable fill and line; line colour is a tint of the child’s own ink, line weight is compose-time morphology — no pixel of the original is ever rewritten. “Current PNG” is a RENDER of the current view; “Original PNG” renders the untouched layer; the creation itself is the JSON, which stays editable.
