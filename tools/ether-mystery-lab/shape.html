Point budget

8 is the production budget; 10–20 are Lab authoring budgets, judged here and refused by the real validator beside the figure. A budget is an authoring target: shrinking it under a bigger figure keeps every light and says the figure exceeds it. Nothing is trimmed, and nothing here changes the runtime.

Tool

Add — click empty sky to place a light. Move — drag a light. Delete — click a light. Join — pressing it joins the lights in their order (1→2→3…), adding only the joins that are missing and removing nothing; then click one light and another to change a join (again to remove; or click a line to remove it). Gap — click a line to mark it missing; click again to restore.
Joins are straight. The Ether figure system has no curved connection, so none is offered here.

What happened on the last generation
The Creature Outline Reference is a simple monochrome silhouette to draw over — composed from the blueprint's features as a visual authoring guide, never the creature. It is drawn under your lights, takes no clicks, is never on the unfinished pane, and has no place in a fixture, a candidate, the preview or the Ether. Suggested points are marked places on the outline — each one a feature or a structural spot (a head, a shoulder, a wing tip, a wing root) — ranked by how diagnostic the blueprint says the feature is, and cut to the budget you have chosen: change the budget and they are recomputed. The budgeted ones are placed as your starting figure when the reference arrives and when the budget grows — real lights, each carrying its feature's name, yours to move, delete or add to; joins and gaps are never made for you, and a light you delete stands as a mark again until you accept it back (a click near it) or press Place suggested points. Choose a feature in the blueprint panel (or accept one of its points) to see that feature's related points in gold. Turn the reference OFF to judge the Ether figure alone: the outline and every suggestion go, your lights and joins stay. A failed or rejected LLM result never becomes a Fixture and never replaces the reference you had.
The flow, in ten steps
  1. Enter a creature
  2. Generate a reference
  3. Choose a budget
  4. Place points over the reference
  5. Move points
  6. Join points
  7. Toggle the reference OFF
  8. Judge
  9. Optionally switch budget
  10. Approve the figure, then save the fixture

Researcher metadata

unsaved figure

Complete — as drawn · over the creature outline

Unfinished — missing joins removed · empty suggested places marked faintly

Both are drawn at one fixed scale for every budget, so figures stay comparable. A missing join shows dashed on the left and is simply absent on the right — which is exactly what a child would meet. While a reference is in use the right pane is the starting figure on the bare sky — the suggested points placed as lights — and any suggested place you have left empty is marked faintly there.

Play in Ether

Opens the real Ether preview with this figure's exact points, joins and gaps: two-tap interaction, completion, and the existing awakening. The creature name never travels; the hint travels beside the figure. Available only where the real Ether can perform the figure.

Approve figure

Freezes the authored figure as the research artifact: points, joins, gaps, the selected budget, and which feature each light was accepted for. Never the outline, the sketch, the blueprint or anything private. Approving activates nothing and publishes nothing — no hint, no gaps, no challenge, no completion, no awakening, no pool entry. Saved with the fixture; any later edit clears it.
