2 Shape

Choose how many points, then add, move and delete them over the reference.
Points
8 is what the Ether performs today; 10–20 are research budgets, judged here and shown as refused beside the figure. Changing the budget never removes a point: shrink it under a bigger figure and every point stays, and the figure is marked as exceeding it until you take points away yourself.
Suggested points are places on the reference — a head, a shoulder, a wing tip — ranked by what the blueprint says matters and cut to the budget. The budgeted ones are placed as your starting figure when the reference arrives and when the budget grows; they are real points from then on, yours to move, delete or add to. A point you delete stands as a faint mark again until you accept it back (a click near it) or press Place suggested points.
Tool
Add — click empty sky. Move — drag a point; its connections and reveal features follow. Delete — click a point; the connections on it go with it.
Undo and Redo cover every point, connection, missing mark and reveal edit (Ctrl/⌘ Z · Shift Z). Reset points removes every point — and with them every connection and every reveal feature standing on one — then places the suggested points again if a reference is showing. Undo brings it all back.

3 Connect

Join the points into a creature. Mark the connections the child will make as missing.
Connect — pressing it connects the points in their order (1→2→3…), adding only what is not yet there and removing nothing. Then click a point and another point: not connected → connected; connected → disconnected. Click a line to select that connection. Missing — click a line to mark it as a connection the child makes (dashed on AUTHOR, absent on JUDGE); click again to restore it. Connections are straight; the Ether figure system has no curved connection, so none is offered here.
Choose the Connect tool, then click a point.
Reset connections removes every connection and missing mark; every point stays exactly where it is. Undo brings them back.

4 Reveal 0 features

PUZZLE = points + connections, what the child completes. REVEAL = the payoff that appears only once the puzzle is whole.
A reveal feature appears ONLY after the creature is completely solved, stays a few seconds, and fades. It is not a point, not a connection, not a missing mark — never connected by a child, never counted toward the budget, never part of completion. It is anchored to your points (move a point and it follows), never to the reference.
Edit a feature in its row — drag its ring on the preview below to move it, shape it with its numbers, delete it with ✕. Reset reveal removes every reveal feature; points and connections are untouched. Undo brings them back.

Author — complete, as drawn · over the creature outline

Judge — unfinished, missing connections removed · empty suggested places marked faintly

AUTHOR is where you work: the reference underneath, your points and connections on top, a missing connection dashed. JUDGE is what a child meets: no reference, no outline, and a missing connection simply absent. Both are drawn at one fixed scale for every budget, so creatures stay comparable. While a reference is showing, any suggested place you have left empty is marked faintly on JUDGE.
