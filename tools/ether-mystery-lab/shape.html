Where the help comes from
Development only. The key is held in memory for this page and nowhere else — never stored, never exported, never logged. Clear it when you are done.
Fixture mode sends nothing anywhere and answers with one generic body plan for every subject — the pipeline, not the creature. The endpoint keeps the provider key in its own environment. Whatever the mode, the only thing sent is the subject you typed plus a fixed authoring contract.

Point budget

8 is the production budget. 12, 16 and 20 are Lab authoring / research budgets: a figure above 8 can be drawn and judged here, and the real validator's refusal is shown beside it. Shrinking a budget under a bigger figure is refused — nothing is trimmed for you. Nothing here changes the runtime.

Tool

Add — click empty sky to place a light. Move — drag a light. Delete — click a light. Join — click one light, then another (again to remove; or click a line to remove it). Gap — click a line to mark it missing; click again to restore.
Joins are straight. The Ether figure system has no curved connection, so none is offered here.
