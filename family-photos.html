Family photos

Share one Google Photos album, and those pictures will be there the next time a story is being made.

Nothing is uploaded. VihuPlanet remembers the link and nothing else. The photos stay in your own Google account, and taking the album down in Google takes it out of here too.

A shared album is shared by link. Anyone who has that link can see those photos. Choose an album you are happy to share that way.

Where the link comes from

  1. Open the album in Google Photos.
  2. Choose Share, then Create link.
  3. Copy it, and paste it in the box above.

This page came from the Magic Card email. There is no account here and nothing to sign in to — you can close it whenever you like, and come back to add another album any time.
